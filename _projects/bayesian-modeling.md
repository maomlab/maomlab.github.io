A principled approach is to develop simulations that model all the decisions, mechanisms, and outcomes. To fit these models we build on recent work in Bayesian statistics and deep learning, explicitly and implicitly differentiating the discrepancy with the observed outcomes back through the simulation to adjust the perturbations and latent parameters.
